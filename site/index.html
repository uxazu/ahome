Features

palette

Material 3 Expressive

Tonal surfaces, big rounded shapes, a clear focus ring and spring motion, by default.

wallpaper

Living wallpapers

21 generated styles and 33 palettes, drawn live and frosted behind your apps.

home

Calm home page

Only your dock, the clock and the weather. Press Down for search and every app.

bolt

Made for weak TVs

Its own render thread for wallpapers, paced remote keys and a tiny 0.6 MB package.

translate

Your language

English by default; follows the system language and ships with 27 translations.

lock

Private

No accounts, no analytics. The only network request is the optional weather line.

Screenshots

arrow_backarrow_forward

Wallpapers that move

Every style is generated from a seed and drawn live on its own thread, so no two homes look alike. Pick one from a grid of previews, or let AHome surprise you.

Make it yours

Dynamic colour is Material 3's way to be personal. Pick a seed and every surface of this page, and of AHome on your TV, is regenerated from it.

Speaks your language

English by default; the interface follows the system language, and you can pick another one in the settings.
